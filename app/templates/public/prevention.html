Monitor Rice Crops Regularly

Regular crop monitoring allows farmers to identify abnormal changes in rice plants and respond before problems become more serious.

  • Inspect leaves and stems.
  • Observe changes in plant color.
  • Record unusual crop conditions.
  • Seek expert advice when necessary.
