{{ lang.public.about.what_is_rice }}

Rice is a cereal grain and one of the most important food crops in the world. It is a staple food for millions of people, particularly in Asia.

Rice is cultivated in different environments, including irrigated lowlands, rainfed lowlands, deepwater areas, and upland fields.
