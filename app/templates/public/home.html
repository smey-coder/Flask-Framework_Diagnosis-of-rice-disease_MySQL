ABOUT CAMBODIAN RICE

Rice: The Heart of Cambodian Agriculture

Rice is one of the most important agricultural crops in Cambodia. It is a major source of food, income, employment, and economic development.

Cambodian farmers have cultivated rice for generations. Rice farming is closely connected to Cambodian culture, traditions, and rural livelihoods.
